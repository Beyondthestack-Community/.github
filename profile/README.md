# Beyond The Stack

Learn. Build. Connect.

Beyond The Stack (BTS) is a technology community for people who are curious
about technology, committed to building, and willing to grow. We bring together
beginners, developers, engineers, creators, and enthusiasts to learn from one
another, build useful things, explore emerging technologies, and create
opportunities together.

We believe technology is bigger than any single language, framework, or
specialization. In a landscape increasingly shaped by AI, the ability to learn,
adapt, think critically, and collaborate matters more than ever.

## What We Do

BTS is organized around four connected areas:

- **Community** — a space to meet people, exchange ideas, ask questions, and
  share knowledge.
- **Learning** — structured learning experiences through BTS Learning, along
  with practical resources and learning paths.
- **Building** — projects, experiments, open source, and real-world solutions
  that turn knowledge into working software.
- **Experiences** — events, workshops, technical sessions, and showcases that
  bring people together.

Different platforms may power different parts of the experience, but the
community is the people, not the platform.

## Who It's For

BTS is not only for experienced developers. Whether you are writing your first
lines of code, learning a new technology, building products, working with data,
exploring AI, automation, or Web3, or already working professionally, there is
room for you here.

## Direction

We are starting with community, learning, events, and building, and we will grow
from there. As BTS develops, we intend to expand into:

- Collaborative and open-source projects
- Mentorship
- Practical, hands-on learning
- Community-led initiatives
- Technology exploration and experimentation

We are not trying to build everything at once. We are building alongside the
people who make BTS what it is.

## This GitHub Organization

This organization holds the technology behind Beyond The Stack, including:

- BTS platforms
- BTS Learning and learning content
- Community tools and automation
- Open-source projects and experiments

Some projects are built by the BTS team; others are built with the community.

## Get Involved

If you are curious, building something, or looking for people to grow with, you
are welcome at Beyond The Stack.
